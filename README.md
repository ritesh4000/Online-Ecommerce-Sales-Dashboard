## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to provide business insights into sales, customers, products, sellers, payments, and delivery performance.

The dashboard helps business managers identify revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, and operational efficiency.

---
# 🎯 Business Problem

The company wants to understand

- Sales performance
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Product category performance
- Delivery performance
- Seller performance
- Revenue growth
- Regional sales trends

This dashboard provides interactive visualizations to answer these business questions.

# 📌 Key Insights

✔ Sales increased during festive seasons.

✔ São Paulo generated the highest revenue.

✔ Electronics contributed the highest sales.

✔ Most customers preferred credit card payments.

✔ Delivery delays were higher in remote regions.

✔ Repeat customers generated significant revenue.
